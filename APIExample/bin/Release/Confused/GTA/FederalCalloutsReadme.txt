INSTALLATION:

Extract the archive to the root of your GTA V directory

HOW TO DO THE CALLOUTS:

Surveil drug deal:
-Press Y to start
-Wait for the buyer and seller to do the transaction (you will know when that happens, they hand stuff to each other). If no transaction happens and the yellow proximity warning area goes away, the intel was bad and there was no deal.

Prevent assassination:
-Go to the search area. Look for people following others and acting strange.
-You will be given a hint right when the callout is accepted telling you the gender of the assassin
-The time it takes the assassin to strike is randomized. It can be anywhere from 1 minute to 5 minutes.

Execute arrest warrant:
Arrest the the person marked by the red blip. They may or may not be armed. Responding code 3 might not be the best idea.

Armored car robbery in progress:
Robbers are attacking an armored car. Get to it and assist the guards in taking out or arresting the robbers. 

Escaped prisoner:
Look for an escaped prisoner and arrest him. Search area will update from time to time.

Kidnapping:
Dispatch has a list of suspect vehicles and is getting reports of more. Pull over each vehicle and look in the back to find the victim.

Bomb Sting:
Watch the van, take the suspect down when he uses his phone. His bomb is fake
And Sometimes the bomb isn't fake but real and will blow up! be careful and mindful of that!
